U8.30 Dependent children of New Zealand citizens or residence class visa holders

  1. Dependent children of New Zealand citizens or residence class visa holders may be granted a student visa, provided that:
    1. the parent intends to stay in New Zealand during the same period of time applied for by the dependent child; and
    2. the parent supports the application; and
    3. either the child’s application for a residence class visa is being considered by INZ or the child’s application for citizenship is being considered by the Department of Internal Affairs, and
  2. A visa under these instructions can be granted for a duration of 12 months:
    1. from the date of arrival, if the applicant is outside New Zealand, or
    2. from the date the visa is granted, if the applicant is in New Zealand.
  3. If the applicant requires longer to obtain the outcome of their residence or citizenship application, immigration officers may consider granting a further visa for sufficient duration to allow their citizenship or residence application to be completed, as long as the requirements of paragraph (a) above continue to be met.
  4. Despite U8.30(a), where the applicant is subject to an overseas adoption order described in section 17(1) and 17(2) of the Adoption Act 1955, a visa cannot, as a result of the adoption, be granted under these instructions unless the adoption is from an ‘exempt country’ (see section 17(3)(b) and (4) of the Adoption Act 1955).
  5. Dependent children of New Zealand citizens or residence class visa holders may be granted student visas without the need to produce evidence of enrolment.
  6. Guarantees of accommodation and/or maintenance for these dependent children are not required as they are supported by a New Zealand citizen or residence class visa holder.

Notes:
- For the purposes of (d), an ‘exempt country’ is one that is listed at Schedule 1AAB of the Adoption Act 1955.
- Where (d) applies, there is no discretion available, either under the Adoption Act 1955, the Immigration Act 2009, or through an ‘exception to instructions’, to grant a visa under U8.30, though an immigration officer may consider granting a visa under other categories of temporary entry instructions.

U8.30.1 Evidence for dependent children of New Zealand citizens or residence class visa holders

  1. Immigration officers must sight evidence of the following:
    1. the New Zealand citizenship or New Zealand residence status of the applicant's parent; and
    2. documents that confirm the applicant's relationship with the parent (see E4.5.10); and
    3. that the applicant and parent are intending to stay in New Zealand during the same period of time; and
    4. that the New Zealand parent supports the application; and
    5. the child has applied for a residence class visa or citizenship.
  2. If the evidence required by (a) above has been previously provided to INZ as part of a residence class visa application from or including the child, an immigration officer can use that evidence to be satisfied the requirements to grant a student visa are met.

Note: Dependent children holding a visa granted under this category may be able to be considered domestic for the purpose of all tuition fees at primary and secondary schools. See section 10(1) of the Education and Training Act 2020 and the associated notice administered by the Ministry of Education for complete information on domestic students.

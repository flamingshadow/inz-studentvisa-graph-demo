U5.1 Status of education providers and programmes

  1. All education providers must certify in their offers of places to foreign students (see U3.5) that the programme of study or training scheme offered complies with foreign student requirements for different kinds of education providers (see U5.5-U5.20).
  2. All education providers offering places to foreign students must be signatories to the Education (Pastoral Care of Tertiary and International Learners) Code of Practice 2021.
  3. Any queries on the status of programmes or training schemes offered by private training establishments should be referred to the Service Delivery Unit, Quality Assurance Division, New Zealand Qualifications Authority (NZQA), PO Box 160, Wellington.
  4. Students will not be granted a student visa to undertake a programme of study or training scheme offered at an education provider that holds a Category Four status under the NZQA’s External Evaluation Review (EER) quality assurance system.
  5. Immigration New Zealand (INZ) may suspend the processing of applications for student visas for study at an education provider where at least one of the following applies:
    1. the offered programme of study or training scheme does not comply with foreign student requirements as set out in U5.5 to U5.20;
    2. the education provider is not complying, or is under investigation by INZ for non-compliance, with its obligations under the Immigration Act, immigration regulations, or immigration instructions;
    3. the education provider is not complying, or is under investigation by education agencies for non-compliance, with its obligations under the Education Act and education regulations including the Education (Pastoral Care of Tertiary and International Learners) Code of Practice 2021;
    4. the education provider is under investigation for a matter that may result in it:
      • no longer being recognised as being able to provide New Zealand qualifications by the relevant regulatory agency; or
      • the programme of study or training scheme the applicant is seeking a visa to study may no longer result in a qualification recognised in New Zealand.

        Note: for the purposes of these instructions, "education agencies" include any agencies identified by the Ministry of Education, as well as the Ministry of Education itself – see Education agencies - Ministry of Education

  6. Where (e) above applies, INZ may:
    1. suspend processing for an initial period of up to six months while awaiting the outcome of the investigation of the provider, and if the investigation has not been completed within that period, extend the initial suspension period to allow for the outcome of the investigation; or
    2. continue processing any such applications where INZ is not satisfied the requirements of immigration instructions have been met and decline the application.
  7. Depending on the nature of the non-compliance or potential non-compliance, processing may be suspended for
    1. all applications to study at the provider; or
    2. applications to study particular programmes at the provider; or
    3. a group of applications to study at the provider that share one or more common characteristics (such as education agent) relevant to the non-compliance or potential non-compliance.
  8. Before a decision is made to suspend the processing of student visas, the following will be taken into account:
    1. evidence of the non-compliance; and
    2. reasons for the non-compliance; and
    3. the duration, frequency, and severity of the non-compliance.
  9. INZ may resume the processing of student visas if it is satisfied that the education provider is complying with its obligations.

U5.1.5 Applicants whose applications are suspended due to provider non-compliance or investigation

  1. If the processing of a student visa application is suspended where U5.1(e) above applies, the applicant may submit an Offer of Place to study with a different education provider (see (c) below) or withdraw their application.
  2. Processing may be suspended on any application that has not been completed through the grant of a visa, decline, or withdrawal by the applicant. For avoidance of doubt this includes applications that have been approved in principle where a visa has not been granted.
  3. Processing may resume if the applicant provides a new Offer of Place for a programme with a different education provider, provided:
    1. the new offer of place meets all relevant requirements for the grant of a student visa; and either
    2. the Offer of Place is for the same programme; or
    3. the immigration officer is satisfied that there is a valid reason for the Offer of Place being for a different programme (for example, the programme is not offered by another provider).

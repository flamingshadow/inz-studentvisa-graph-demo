U16.5 Currency and conditions

  1. Despite U13.15, a student visa holder may be granted under these instructions with conditions allowing work for up to 25 hours in any given week during the validity of the visa.
  2. Visas can be granted up to a maximum period of six months with multiple entry travel conditions.
  3. Where the education provider is continuing to operate the original programme of study, education provider and location must be specified on the visa.
  4. In the event the education provider has ceased to operate, the visa can be granted without a specified programme of study, education provider, or location.

Effective 03/11/2025
